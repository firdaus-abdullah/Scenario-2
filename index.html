Activity 2 — Sequence & Spot the Error

👥 Groups of 3–4 ⏱ 10 minutes 🃏 Sequence cards 🎤 Debrief 🏆 10 points
  1. Your facilitator will provide shuffled gowning step cards for an aseptic gowning procedure.
  2. Arrange the cards in the correct order — donning sequence, top-down approach.
  3. Identify 3 common gowning errors that could compromise sterility.
  4. Explain why each error matters — link to EU GMP Annex 1 requirements.
  5. Present your sequence and findings to the class — 2 minutes per group.

🎤 Debrief — Present Your Findings

Each group has 2 minutes to present their sequence and findings. Use these prompts to structure your debrief:

  • Show your final sequence — explain why you placed each step where you did.
  • Identify your 3 errors and explain why each one compromises sterility.
  • Link each error to the relevant EU GMP Annex 1 requirement.
  • Propose a practical CAPA for each error — what would you change in the procedure or training?
  • Compare with other groups — did anyone sequence or interpret a step differently?
